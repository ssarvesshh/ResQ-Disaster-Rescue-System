DATA STRUCTURES IN ACTION

DS Workbench

The ResQ prototype demonstrates these concepts through incident and team operations.

1. Binary Max-HeapPrioritizes emergency records by score. The root contains the highest-priority incident.
2. Circular QueueStores available rescue team IDs and assigns teams in FIFO order.
3. Hash MapMaps incident IDs to incident objects for direct record lookup.
4. StackStores previous application states. Undo restores the most recent saved state first.
5. ArraysStore incident records, rescue teams and activity history.
6. Searching & SortingFinds incidents by keyword and sorts cases by priority score.

Workflow: Register incident → Calculate priority → Add to heap → Assign team from queue → Update status → Record activity.
